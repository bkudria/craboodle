# Living Room Rules

This space is run on a [living room rules](https://drmaciver.com/notebook/2020/07/living-room-rules/) principle: it is operated by me, Ben Kudria, largely on my terms, and you're here as my guest. Please play nicely and help keep the vibe pleasant. In return, I'll do my best to be easygoing and fair while keeping the space healthy.

## The short version

- **Enhance the vibe.**
- **Don't be a jerk.**

Both are arbitrated by me.

## What that means in practice

For most people, as long as you're behaving well and going along with the general vibe, there will be little to no moderation. If you're doing something I think is bad for the space, I'll usually just nudge you. If nudging doesn't work, or if someone's being a real jerk, I'll ask them to leave.

I will sometimes exclude people more or less arbitrarily if they make the space worse for me or for others. I'd rather not use this power often, but sometimes it's necessary — including for behavior elsewhere.

## Things I will come down on hard

- Anything I interpret as bigotry, prejudice, rudeness, or unkindness.
- Harassment, particularly after someone has asked you to stop.
- Threats, endangerment, or sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## If something isn't working

If something about the space isn't working for you — including something I'm doing — please tell me at ben@kudria.net and we'll talk about it.
